# Photon fork vs KappaPT v18.8 — RT/PT and lighting comparison

**Last updated:** 2026-11-03 after the Gemini cross-check and the user's PT-only product decision. Active roadmap: `docs/PT_ONLY_FORK_PLAN.md`.  
**Scope:** only ray/path tracing, indirect/direct lighting, shadows, AO, reflections/refractions, emissive light transport, BRDFs, volumetric lighting, and the temporal/spatial reconstruction needed by RT/PT. Sky art, cloud modelling, weather, exposure, tone mapping, bloom, DoF and general post-processing are intentionally excluded.

## Bottom line

- **Photon fork is a gameplay-oriented hybrid raster + world-space voxel RT renderer.** Its `LIGHTING_MODE_RT` path has voxel RTGI, a toroidal DDGI-style probe cache, voxel RT AO and voxel RT reflections. Direct light still uses raster shadow maps. It deliberately has no SSGI/SSR feedback.
- **The fork's `LIGHTING_MODE_PT` profiles are not a path tracer.** Per `shaders/settings.glsl`, `shaders.properties` and `diffuse_lighting.glsl`, they are a reserved direct-only, GI-off A/B baseline: no GI, no RT AO, and RT resources are not allocated. Do not compare these profiles as a second renderer.
- **KappaPT v18.8 is a heavier hybrid path tracer.** It combines screen-space path tracing, a voxel octree, L1 spherical-harmonic diffuse irradiance, a world-space SH irradiance cache, multi-bounce specular infrastructure and separate diffuse/specular accumulation+denoising. Primary visibility and a substantial part of direct lighting remain rasterized; diffuse tracing is one explicit bounce with the cache approximating further bounces.
- **Best architectural decision:** keep Photon's stable, world-space clipmap renderer as the **PT scene backend** and selectively port *ideas*, not Kappa's pipeline wholesale. Kappa's world0 pipeline alone has 37 `.csh` compute wrappers. The highest-value ideas are reconstruction-first: adaptive accumulation, multi-scale denoising, directional SH irradiance, dedicated specular history and reactive masks.
- **Product decision changed on 2026-11-03:** ship **PT-only**. There will be no separate RT gameplay mode, OFF mode, HYBRID mode or GI-off `pt_*` placeholder. All profiles become PT quality tiers. Keep Photon's lighting/voxel/emitter substrate, delete the RT/PT split, and rebuild temporal accumulation + denoising first. See `docs/PT_ONLY_FORK_PLAN.md`.
- **Licensing:** Kappa's `LICENSE` permits private modifications, but §4 requires explicit permission from RRe36 to use parts of its source code in another original project; §§1–3 restrict public redistribution, require credit and the same license for public modified versions, and prohibit monetization without permission. Study the design and write a clean-room implementation, or obtain permission before copying code/assets. The local `KappaPT_v18.8.zip` is ignored by `.gitignore` and should not be committed or redistributed.

## 1. Architecture and light transport

Legend: **+** advantage, **−** limitation.

| Area / technology | Photon fork — included implementation | Photon pros / cons | KappaPT v18.8 — included implementation | Kappa pros / cons |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Sources: `settings.glsl`, `program/d4_deferred_shading.fsh`, `include/lighting/diffuse_lighting.glsl`. | **+** Stable, predictable, world-space indirect transport; no previous-frame color feedback; explicit GI-off baseline. **−** Not a unified path tracer; `PT` labels are misleading; indirect transport is mainly diffuse. | Raster primary/direct pass plus `PTLight.comp` diffuse path tracing, L1 SH output, `PTReflections.comp` specular transport and a 3D SH irradiance cache. `deferred15.fsh` composites `albedo × (direct + PT SH + emission)`. | **+** Real stochastic indirect transport; directional irradiance; separate specular path. **−** Still hybrid, not unbiased full PT; raster direct light is separate; one explicit diffuse bounce; cache supplies most “multi-bounce”. |
| **Lighting modes / profiles** | Legacy `low/medium/high/ultra/extreme` plus `rt_low/rt_medium/rt_high` and `pt_low/pt_medium/pt_high`; mode is profile-driven, not a menu switch. | **World geometry representation** | Three nested `R32UI` toroidal clipmaps (`voxel`, `voxel_mid`, `voxel_coarse`), 64/96/128³ per profile; level scales 1/2/4 blocks, reaching 128/256/512 blocks across at 128³. | **Ray traversal / visibility** | DDA through fine→mid→coarse clipmaps whose cell scales are 1/2/4 blocks, first-hit shading, transmittance across glass levels, sub-box ray-AABB intersection and `trace_any_hit` for AO/probe occlusion. Sun and sky visibility at bounce hits are voxel marches, not shadow-map lookups. | **+** Camera-independent, world-space sun visibility; no raster cascade seams in GI; same geometry for GI, AO and reflections. **−** 48-step/48-block sun-visibility budget; no occupancy BVH; coarse levels are scale-only; level-boundary logic is intricate. | `RVoxel_March` DDA and `RVoxel_MarchBVH` with `maxLevel = 8 - 1 = 7` and `2^level` stride leaps through an `R8UI` occupancy mip; `shadow/vertex.vsh` writes up to 8 bitplanes. `OCTREE_LEVELS=4` in settings is unreferenced. Optional SSPT first hit (`SSPT_Enabled`), then voxel fallback; `maxTraversalDistance` check is commented out in `PTLight.comp`. | **+** Octree skips large empty regions; SSPT captures non-cubic/entity screen detail. **−** Two representations can disagree; SSPT fails off-screen/behind camera; distance limit and `OCTREE_LEVELS` slider are not wired. |
| **Diffuse GI / path-traced indirect estimator** | `rtgi_trace.csh`: cosine hemispheres, 2/4/8 rays per pixel at quarter/half/full resolution. Output is RGB mean radiance + sky-origin fraction. Deferred `diffuse_lighting.glsl` consumes it as mean incoming radiance with no receiver-normal evaluation; the forward/translucent DDGI path uses `bent_normal` only for backface/occlusion weighting. The mean direction stored in `ddgi_direction_img` is not used for directional shading. | **+** Off-screen valid, no screen-space feedback, explicit sky/local split, strong leak control, no analytic ambient fallback. **−** Isotropic RGB radiance, not directional irradiance; normal maps do not react directionally to RTGI; 2 rays and half-res by default; one local bounce plus a probe term. | `PTLight.comp`: `PT_SPP` cosine paths, plausible-hemisphere sampling, Hammon diffuse BRDF throughput, SSPT or voxel-BVH hit, emission + direct light + irradiance cache at hit, skybox miss. Returns four-coefficient L1 SH for the first-bounce direction: `IrradianceToSH` packs `vec4(L11, L1_1, L10, L00) * Y` plus `vec2(Co, Cg)`. `useIrradianceCache` and `RIrradianceCache_UseSH` are active; `deferred15.fsh` evaluates `ProjectIrradiance(SH, Gbuffer0.SceneNormal)` with a normal-mapped `SceneNormal` when `normalmapEnabled` is on (default). | **+** Directional irradiance reacts to normal maps; physically motivated throughput; detailed screen-space first hits. **−** 1 SPP default demands heavy accumulation; screen-space fallback artefacts; no minimum ambient fallback (the minimum-ambient code is commented out). |
| **Multi-bounce / irradiance cache** | Fixed 32³ toroidal DDGI cache, default 2-block spacing (64-block extent), 2048 probes/frame, 16-frame full cycle, 8/16/32/64 rays per probe, 0.80 hysteresis, shape-aware relocation, scroll invalidation, trilinear/backface weighting, coverage fade and query-time voxel occlusion. Stores isotropic RGB irradiance + sky fraction. `rtgi_probe_update.csh` does not read its own output in the same dispatch. | **+** Cheap second bounce, works for forward/translucent surfaces, explicit no-same-dispatch-feedback contract, strong wall-leak prevention. **−** No directionality, 16-frame refresh/churn, only 64-block extent at default spacing, complex invalidation/relocation. | 128³ L1 SH irradiance cache packed into a 256×256×128 `RGBA16F` image plus a flip image; `cache/generate.comp` traces one cosine ray per cell/frame, blends SH coefficients with history, then `reproject.comp`/`filter.comp` scroll and spatially filter the cache. Path tracer reads it at each hit. There are no recursive `bounceNumber` bounces and `cacheSPP` is unused; `generate.comp` traces exactly one cosine ray per cell per frame. | **+** Directional SH, world-space cache, reprojection and spatial filtering, effective multibounce approximation. **−** Very large memory footprint, lag/bleeding, per-frame cache update cost; no recursive multibounce; `cacheSPP`, `radianceVolumeSPP`, `radianceVolumeUpdate` and `bounceNumber` are not wired in v18.8. |
| **Specular reflections** | `rt_reflections.glsl` + `specular_lighting.glsl`: world-space voxel RT for all eligible surfaces, GGX VNDF for rough surfaces (`RT_REFLECTION_RAYS=1/2/4`), one mirror ray for smooth surfaces, sky-map miss, tinted-glass transmittance and a 64-luminance firefly clamp. No SSR or previous-composite sampling. PT placeholder is environment-only. | **+** Off-screen and behind-camera reflections; consistent voxel scene; works in all worlds and with `SHADOW` disabled. **−** One specular bounce; no dedicated reflection history or denoiser; noisy at 1–2 rough rays. | Dedicated `PTReflections.comp` at 0.5×1.0 resolution; `reflectionMultibounce` has `SPECULAR_BOUNCES=2`, GGX VNDF with tail clamping, Fresnel throughput, direct light/emission/cache at hits, sky misses and TIR-aware refraction hooks. Separate `RefAccumulate.comp` + `PT_DenoiseRef.comp`/SPF filter. Default `resourcepackReflectionsEnabled` and `voxelPT_ReflectionsEnabled` are off: the loop breaks after the first bounce, the voxel fallback is inactive, and ray-traced output is mainly used for water/wet surfaces. General materials receive direct GGX highlights. | **+** Multi-bounce-capable framework, dedicated temporal+roughness-guided denoise, reactive mask, physically detailed Fresnel. **−** Defaults force one bounce and narrow material coverage; full resource-pack/voxel reflections are disabled; SSR path has usual visibility limitations; many passes. |
| **Refraction / transmission** | Screen-space UV refraction in `c1_blend_layers.fsh`, depth validity test, `REFRACTION_OFF/ALL/WATER_ONLY`, `REFRACTION_INTENSITY`; G-buffer `colortex17` stores stained-glass optical filter; voxel DDA multiplies `ray_transmittance` by `mat.albedo` for stained glass IDs 81–96 and passes through clear glass ID 80, so colored glass is not skipped or treated as opaque. | **+** Cheap, TAAU-aware, integrated with voxel transmittance and translucent layers. **−** No true refracted ray, TIR, or off-screen geometry; screen-space refraction only; voxel transmittance is a per-cell albedo multiply rather than path-length Beer-Lambert. | `REFRACTIONS_MODE=1` (“Simple”) by default; mode 2 enables `RefractionTrace` with `refract`, `RefractionEta`, TIR, screen-space first hit and `VOXEL_REFRACTIONS` BVH fallback; `SCREENSPACE_REFRACTIONS` is also defined. ## 3. Reconstruction, performance, diagnostics and compatibility

| Area / technology | Photon fork — included implementation | Photon pros / cons | KappaPT v18.8 — included implementation | Kappa pros / cons |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sampling / noise** | R2 low-discrepancy sequences, `noisetex`/blue-noise dithering, frame-indexed probe rays, deterministic `safe_ray_dir` without angular jitter, `RTGI_MAX_RADIANCE=12` per-ray clamp, `EMISSION_ANCHOR=8`, reflection clamp at luminance 64. | **+** Strong, explicit firefly control; deterministic seams; emitter anchor prevents legitimate emitters being clipped. **−** Low default ray count makes temporal/filter quality critical. | Temporal 3D blue noise, R2/quasirandom sampling, `frame255` seed, gradient noise, `PT_SPP` slider, `clamp16F` and NaN guards; reflective caustics and path guiding are disabled. | **+** Rich sample decorrelation; high SPP ceiling (up to 16). **−** 1 SPP default; no explicit absolute radiance clamp comparable to Photon’s anchor; more tuning surfaces. |
| **Reservoir / directional reuse (ReSTIR and guiding)** | `shaders/photonics/` restores upstream compatibility stubs, including `restir_gi_modifier.glsl` (`color *= SKYLIGHT_I / blocklight_scale`) and `write_indirect()`. `photonics.glsl` and `ph_samplers.glsl` are empty placeholders replaced by the Photonics mod at runtime. The README confirms **no live `/photonics/` include**, no menu entry, no `PHOTONICS_*` define, and no support for running external ReSTIR GI alongside `rtgi_*`. | **+** A documented external-mod interface exists without double-counting indirect light; zero runtime cost in the fork. **−** No active ReSTIR reservoir, no light-ID ownership, and the voxel injection anchor moved to `shadow.vsh`; re-enabling is a design task, not a toggle. | No Photonics/ReSTIR integration. `PT_GUIDING` is commented out (the active guide prepasses only use its code when enabled); `PT_GuidePrepass_Reproject.comp` and `PT_GuideFlip.comp` have no wrapper includes. | **+** Guiding scaffolding is present for experimentation. **−** No working directional reuse; disabled code and dead files add maintenance noise. |
| **Temporal accumulation** | `rtgi_temporal.csh`: 9-tap neighbourhood reprojection, depth + octahedral-normal disocclusion, confidence-weighted pure `1/n` blend capped at `RTGI_HISTORY_FRAMES=32`, M2 luminance moment for variance seeded from a 3×3 spatial sigma on reset, absolute radiance cap 32, no variance clip or neighbour-relative history clamp, packed sky-weight/age, bilinear radiance history, soft 10%-history disocclusion reset and ping-pong images. | **+** Very deliberate anti-ghosting design; no motion-driven alpha reset on pans; no variance clip avoids brightness pumping; variance moments correctly track E[x²], not (EMA)². **−** 32-frame cap limits static convergence; no separate reflection history; no adaptive accumulation strength. | Separate `DiffAccumulate.comp` and `RefAccumulate.comp`; SH history + variance/age aux; depth/camera-motion rejection, adaptive `ADAPT_STRENGTH`, `minAccumMult`, `maxFrames=256`, 7×7 spatial fallback when history invalid, reflection reactive masks for water/wetness. | **+** Much longer convergence, separate diffuse/specular lifetimes, adaptive responsiveness. **−** More history images and ghosting risk; complex packed SH/aux layout; hard-to-tune. |

**Revised priority:** after the PT-only decision, the execution order is governed by `docs/PT_ONLY_FORK_PLAN.md`. ### Already present in Photon — do not re-port

- **Variance-guided SVGF-style edge stopping is already implemented.** `rtgi_temporal.csh` computes a 3×3 spatial sigma, seeds and EMA-tracks an M2 luminance moment, and `rtgi_filter.csh` sets `sigma_l = RTGI_FILTER_PHI * sqrt(variance)` with normal/depth/luminance weights. Gemini's proposed “add SVGF variance weighting” is not a missing feature.
- **TAAU-aware reprojection, 9-tap neighbourhood search, octahedral-normal disocclusion, sky/local separation and the full GI debug ladder** are already present; do not replace them with Kappa's history layout just to gain variance tracking.
- **Shape-aware sub-block DDA and tinted glass transmittance** already exist. The missing piece is path-length/extinction accumulation, not the entire Kappa atlas/absorption stack.
- **Photonics / ReSTIR hooks are intentionally inactive**, not an unfinished feature; enabling them without deciding who owns direct and indirect light would double-count GI.

### Suggested implementation order

1. **PT-only contract and baseline:** remove `LIGHTING_MODE_RT`, OFF/HYBRID and `pt_*` placeholders; decide whether to port fetched performance commit `4dd4391`; record matched captures and FPS.
2. **Reconstruction data contract:** aux channels, per-pixel SPP, static-state detector, reset reasons and debug views.
3. **Temporal accumulation rewrite:** adaptive history, longer cap, invalid-history preaccumulation fallback, bounded ghosting.
4. **Spatial denoise redesign:** wide→narrow multi-scale strides, guide/variance prepasses, converged bypass and square-bloom footprint fix. **Do not re-tune φ/σ.**
5. **Directional SH and separate specular reconstruction:** L1 SH diffuse, then specular history/reactive masks/SPF-like filter and multi-bounce reflections.
6. **Explicit PT integrator:** diffuse reference, NEE/MIS, GGX-VNDF, Russian roulette and numerical acceptance on Photon's clipmaps.
7. **Cleanup and release gates:** UI/lang/docs/tests, image budget, static/runtime/performance validation.

This supersedes the earlier RT-first order. See `docs/PT_ONLY_FORK_PLAN.md` for gates, legal boundary and work packages.

## 7. PT-only product decision and active roadmap

On 2026-11-03 the user decided to **ship PT only**. The separate RT gameplay mode is no longer worth maintaining: after the force-pushed workload reduction Photon is reported at 100–120 FPS at stock x1.0, versus 60 FPS before and 90 FPS for KappaPT v18.8. The remaining problem is not the lighting model; it is the final processing — accumulation, temporal reconstruction and denoising.

Active roadmap: **`docs/PT_ONLY_FORK_PLAN.md`**.

Its order is:

1. PT-only contract, baseline and branch-sync decision.
2. Reconstruction data contract and static-state detector.
3. Temporal accumulation rewrite.
4. Spatial denoise redesign; no more weight tuning.
5. L1 SH diffuse and separate specular reconstruction.
6. Explicit PT integrator on Photon's clipmap backend.
7. Profiles, docs, tests and release gates.

Kappa is used as a **conceptual reference only**. No code or assets may be copied without RRe36's permission; even with permission, Kappa's SH/history/atlas/octree contracts would require a clean-room rewrite against Photon's 32-bit clipmaps and RGB + sky-fraction pipeline.

**Repository changes made for this report:** the uploaded `.txt` was renamed/copied to the workspace as `KappaPT_v18.8.zip` (ignored by `*.zip` in `.gitignore`); this comparison document was added and then updated with the Gemini cross-check and PT-only decision; `docs/PT_ONLY_FORK_PLAN.md` was added. No shader code was modified, no branch was switched, and the fetched performance commit was not merged.
